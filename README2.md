# 🔒 [authlocal.org](https://authlocal.org/)

**authlocal is a free login system.**  
any website can ask for you to sign-in via authlocal.  
manage your identities any time at https://authlocal.org/  

**own your identity.**  
each identity you create has a permanent seed key. don't lose it. don't share it. it's forever yours.

- 🔑 **cryptographic** — passwordless, usernameless, emailless.  
- 🏡 **local-only** — keys stay on your device (no cloud databases).  
- 🗽 **user-sovereign** — control, backup, and transfer your keys however you wish.  
- 🥷 **privacy-focused** — identities are pseudonymous and without personal information.  
- 💖 **free and open-source** — service provided for free at global scale.
